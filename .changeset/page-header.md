Add `PageHeader` for the top of a content page. It renders a `title` heading (`titleLevel`, default `1`), an optional `description` and an optional `actions` area that sits beside the title on wide screens and wraps under it on narrow ones. Empty `description` and `actions` render nothing. It is separate from the app bar (`Header`) and card headers. New exported types: `PageHeaderProps`, `PageHeaderTitleLevel`.
